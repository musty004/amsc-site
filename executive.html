    Executive Committee

    The students who lead AMSC for the 1448 AH / 2026–2027 session, and what each portfolio is responsible for.

    What the Executive Committee does

    The Executive Committee provides leadership and coordination for AMSC. It plans the session, oversees our programmes, guides decisions and makes sure the community’s goals are carried out.

    It is made up of students who give their time, skills and commitment alongside their studies, across a brothers’ wing led by the Ameer and a sisters’ wing led by the Ameerah. Most portfolios have an officer on each side, so every member has someone to go to.

    • Plans the session’s programmes and calendar
    • Represents AMSC to the Institute and seeks approval for programmes
    • Coordinates the committees and volunteers who run each programme
    • Keeps the records, the accounts and the membership register
    • Looks after the welfare of members, especially new students
