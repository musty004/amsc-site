    Prayer times

    Today’s prayer times for Kaduna, jama’ah times, and where we pray: daily, on Fridays and on Eid.

    Today in Kaduna

    Jama’ah is held 15 minutes after each prayer time, except Dhuhr, which is fixed at 1:15pm. Prayer times update each day and are saved on your device, so this page still works without data.

    Where we pray

    Daily prayers on campus, Jumu’ah at the Base Central Mosque, and Eid at the Air Force Primary School.

    Every Friday

    Jumu’ah

    Nigerian Air Force Base Central Mosque, beside the NAF Hospital

    We observe Jumu’ah together at the Base Central Mosque. Come early so you can find a place and listen to the khutbah from the beginning.

    Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha

    Eid prayer

    Usually at the Air Force Primary School

    The exact time for Eid prayer is announced by the Imam at the Base Mosque. It is also shared with members on the AMSC WhatsApp Community, so register to receive it.

    Daily prayers on campus

    AFIT Mosque

    • Brothers and sisters, separate sections
    • All five daily prayers in congregation
    • Islamiyyah and the Sisters Circle

    The main mosque on campus and the home of the community. Wudu facilities available.

    Male Hostel Mosque

    • Brothers
    • Daily prayers, and Musaffa on Wednesdays

    The closest option for brothers in the hostel, especially for Fajr and Isha.

    Prayer spaces near lectures

    • Dhuhr and Asr between classes
    • Ask any AMSC member for directions

    Know a space that should be listed here? Tell the media team and we will add it.

    Qiblah at AFIT: roughly east-north-east. Every listed space is already aligned, so follow the lines on the mat rather than a phone compass indoors, where metal and walls throw the reading off.

    The etiquette of Friday

    Jumu’ah is the gathering of the week. The recommended practice for the day: ghusl before setting out, clean or best clothes, perfume if you have it, arriving early, reciting Surah Al-Kahf, sending abundant salawat on the Prophet ﷺ, and listening to the khutbah without disturbing anyone.

    Jumu’ah location

    Nigerian Air Force Base Central Mosque, beside the NAF Hospital.
