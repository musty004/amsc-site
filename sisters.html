    Sisters

    The sisters’ wing of AMSC: the Sisters Circle, the online Ta’alim, World Hijab Day, welfare, and a full share in everything the community does.

    A space of your own

    The sisters’ wing has its own gatherings, its own teaching circle, its own leaders under the Ameerah, and its own part in every major event AMSC holds.

    Two things run every week. The Sisters Circle meets in person on Friday afternoons at the female section of the AFIT Masjid. The Ta’alim meets online twice a week in the evening, so you can attend from the hostel or from home during the break.

    This week

    • Ta’alim: Monday and Wednesday, 8:00pm, Google Meet
    • Sisters Circle: Friday, 4:00pm to 6:00pm, AFIT Masjid
    • Islamiyyah: Saturday and Sunday, 10:00am

    World Hijab Day 2026

    More Than A Scarf

    Each year the sisters’ wing marks World Hijab Day with talks, conversation and a shared gathering, about what the hijab means, what it asks and what it is worth, told by the women who wear it.

    Join the Circle

    What the Circle covers

    Across a semester, part learning, part skills and part simply being together.

    Fiqh for women

    Purity, prayer and fasting, hijab, and the questions people are shy to ask in a mixed room.

    Health and wellbeing

    Sessions led by women with the right training, including how and where to get proper help.

    Skills and enterprise

    Practical vocational sessions and small business basics, with space to sell what you make.

    Study and career

    Managing demanding coursework, and hearing from Muslim women working in technical fields.

    Safety

    Recognising harassment, documenting it, and the routes available for reporting and support.

    Company

    Games, quizzes, picnics, food and conversation. A community you enjoy is a community you keep.

    Part of everything AMSC does

    Sisters compete in the Musabaqah, serve on the committees, run the indoor games during sports weeks, write for the blog, and sit on the Executive Committee through the Ameerah and her team.

    Sisters can reach the sisters’ welfare officers directly on WhatsApp.

    Photographs and privacy

    AMSC does not publish a photograph of a sister without her explicit permission. If an image of you has been shared and you want it removed, contact the media team and it comes down without discussion.
