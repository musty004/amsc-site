# AFIT Muslim Students' Community (AMSC)
Official website of the AFIT Muslim Students' Community, Air Force Institute of Technology, Kaduna.
Together in Faith and Purpose.
Live site: https://afitmsc.com.ng

## About
AMSC is a faith-driven student body that supports Muslim students at AFIT through worship,
learning, welfare and service. This website carries our programmes, prayer times, the Executive
Committee, the freshers' guide, the gallery and ways to register and get in touch.
