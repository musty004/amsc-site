    One Ummah.One Campus.

    The AFIT Muslim Students’ Community is a faith-driven body dedicated to fostering spiritual growth, unity and purpose among Muslim students at the Air Force Institute of Technology.

    Rooted in Islamic values and guided by sincerity, discipline, faith and brotherhood, we give students a place to grow in deen and character through worship, service, learning and positive impact on campus. We are more than a religious group: we help one another succeed academically, socially and personally.

    Faith, knowledge, skills and care

    Our programmes cover four areas of student life. Each is run by its own team of student volunteers.

    Faith and the Qur’an

    Weekly Islamiyya classes in Qur’an, Arabic, Fiqh and Tawheed, a recitation circle, Da’wah Week and the annual Musabaqatul Qur’an.

    • Islamiyya
    • Musaffa
    • Da’wah Week
    • Musabaqatul Qur’an

    Academic excellence

    Weekend tutorials taught by students who have passed the course, a shared past question bank, and study materials through our partnership with PDFNest.

    Digital skills

    Led by students, for students

    The Executive Committee plans the session, oversees our programmes and makes sure AMSC’s goals are carried out.

    Its members are students who give their time and skills alongside their own studies, across a brothers’ wing and a sisters’ wing.
