    Library

    Notes, reading lists and reference material from AMSC programmes, kept in one place so nothing is lost when a session ends.

    What is in the library

    Islamiyyah notes

    Notes, books and audio from the weekend classes, by subject: Tawheed, Fiqh, Seerah and Arabic. Registered Islamiyyah students download them from the Student Portal.

    Open the Student Portal Or ask the Mudeer (opens in a new tab)

    Qur’an resources

    Tajwid guides, memorisation plans and the Musaffa reading schedule.

    About Musaffa

    Past questions

    The academic collection lives in its own shared folder, sorted by department and level.

    Open the past question bank (opens in a new tab)

    Reading lists

    Short, checked lists for people at different stages: three books, not thirty, so you might finish one.

    See the lists

    Book shelf

    Donated books held by the community and lent to members on a signed register. Return what you borrow.

    Donate a book

    Reading lists by stage

    Specific titles are recommended by the Da’wah team and the Islamiyyah teachers, and checked before anything is added.

    New to studying

    Foundations

    • A clear translation of the Qur’an with brief commentary
    • A short, reliable text on the basics of belief
    • A concise Seerah of the Prophet ﷺ

    Building

    Practice and character

    • A fiqh manual covering purification and prayer
    • A collection of forty hadith with explanation
    • A work on purification of the heart

    Going further

    Depth

    • Tafsir of selected surahs
    • Arabic grammar for readers of the Qur’an
    • Islamic history beyond the early period

    Help the library grow

    The fastest way to make this collection useful is for members to add to it. Two things are always welcome:

    • Notes: if you take good notes at Islamiyyah, send a scan.
    • Books: physical copies you have finished with, for the shelf.
    Offer to contribute

    How material is checked

    Nothing is added on a whim. Anything that teaches religion is reviewed by the Da’wah team first, and where scholars differ, the collection presents the mainstream positions rather than a single verdict.

    If you think something in the library is wrong or poorly sourced, tell us and it will be looked at properly.
