    AMAN alumni network

    The AFIT Muslim Alumni Network brings together all AFIT Muslim graduates to foster brotherhood, unity and networking.

    Officially launched

    A bond that follows you

    The connection between AMSC members travels far. Pioneers and founding members of the community still meet in Abuja and elsewhere, years after leaving Kaduna, while serving in different places.

    AMAN makes that deliberate: a register, a way to reach one another, and a way for graduates to keep supporting the students who came after them.

    Brotherhood, unity, networking

    Three words from the launch, and the whole purpose. Members are AFIT Muslim graduates wherever they now are: in service, in postgraduate study, at work or at home.

    What alumni do for AMSC

    • Mentor students in their own department or field
    • Support welfare, the Musabaqah and Ramadan iftar
    • Speak at orientation, career sessions and the sendforth
    • Pass on internships, NYSC advice and job openings

    Join the alumni register

    Graduating members are inducted at the FYB Sendforth Walimah, and the register is open to anyone who passed through AMSC at any point.

    Mentor a student

    One student, one conversation a month, matched by department and by what they need.

    Sign up to mentor

    Jobs and internships

    Openings shared by alumni go to the community. If you can place a student somewhere, tell us.

    Share an opening

    Give back

    A standing contribution, however small, is worth more to the welfare fund than one large gift a year.

    How members are sent forth

    The FYB Sendforth Walimah closes each session for final-year students.

    Networking

    Final-year students, the community they served with, and invited guests.

    Inspiring sessions

    A keynote address and an impact panel discussion, with time for questions.

    FYB awards

    Recognition for the finalists, followed by games, refreshments and giveaways.

    Induction into the alumni network, with contact details recorded on the day.
