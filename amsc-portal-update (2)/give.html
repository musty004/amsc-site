    What we are raising for

    People give to specific things. Each project has a purpose, a target published when it opens, and a report when it is done.

    Open

    Musabaqah prize fund

    Prizes and certificates for every category of the annual Qur’an competition.

    Islamiyyah materials

    Copies of the Qur’an, whiteboards, printed notes and teaching materials for the weekend classes.

    Open

    Ramadan iftar

    Daily iftar for students who stay on campus through the month, and the Annual Ramadan Lecture and Iftar.

    How funds are accounted for

    Accounts are kept by the Financial Officer and the Financial Secretary and presented to members at the general meeting. Two signatures are needed for every disbursement above the agreed limit, and every payment has a receipt.

    How funds are used

    • Programmes: Islamiyyah materials, tutorial printing and venue costs.
    • Welfare: direct support to members in need.
    • Events: the Musabaqah, orientation, Eid gatherings and the sendforth.
    • Media: printing, design and this website.

    Our rules on money

    • No single person can move funds alone
    • Every disbursement is recorded with a receipt
    • Accounts published monthly, without being asked
    • Welfare reported as totals only, never names
    • Zakat held and given out separately

    Graduates: keep the door open behind you
