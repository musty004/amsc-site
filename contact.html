    Frequently asked

    What does AMSC stand for?

    The AFIT Muslim Students’ Community: the body serving Muslim students at the Air Force Institute of Technology, Kaduna.

    Who can join?

    Every Muslim student of the Institute, at any level and in any department. The IDIT training cohorts are also open to people outside AFIT.

    How do I pay my dues?

    Through the payment link on the Give page. Dues are ₦1,000 and above per semester.

    Can I get a letter of attestation for a scholarship?

    Yes. Email the General Secretaries at Afitmssn@gmail.com with your name, department, level and the scholarship, and allow a few days.

    How do I invite AMSC to an event or propose a partnership?

    Email Afitmssn@gmail.com, addressed to the Ameer. Programmes involving external guests go through Institute clearance, so allow time.
