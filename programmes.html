    Islamiyya

    Classes focused on the Qur’an, Arabic, Fiqh, Tawheed and Islamic knowledge.

    The core teaching programme of the community runs on both weekend mornings for ninety minutes. Classes are streamed by level, so a complete beginner and someone who has studied for years both sit in the right room. Bring a notebook, and come on time.

    • Saturdays and Sundays
    • 10:00am to 11:30am
    • AFIT Mosque

    What is taught

    • Qur’an reading and tajwid, from the very beginning
    • Arabic letters, reading and basic grammar
    • Fiqh of purification, prayer, fasting and zakat
    • Tawheed, the foundations of belief
    • Seerah and the lives of the companions

    Tutorials

    Academic support for students who may be struggling with their studies.

    Tutorials are taught by students who have already passed the course: Engineering Mathematics, Applied Mechanics, Fluid Mechanics, Programming, Chemistry and more. Come with your notes; the rooms are booked for fixed slots. The timetable for this session is being prepared.

    Academic support

    Also from the academics team

    • A free past question bank
    • Letters of attestation for scholarship applications
    • Exam-season revision plans and group study
    • Study materials through our partnership with PDFNest

    Fridays 4:00pm to 6:00pm, AFIT Masjid (female section)

    Sisters Circle

    A student club for Muslim sisters, designed to foster sisterhood, support, connection and personal development.

    Each week brings vocational training, quizzes, games, interactive sessions and the occasional picnic. Alongside the Circle, the sisters’ Ta’alim meets online on Mondays and Wednesdays at 8:00pm for Fiqh and Tawheed, so sisters can join from the hostel or from home.

    Islamic Digital and IT Training

    IDIT

    Technology and digital skills training designed to empower students.

    A four-week online programme run during the holidays, designed for complete beginners and open beyond AFIT. The third cohort, IDIT 3.0, began on 15 December 2025 and graduated on 21 February 2026 with a ceremony themed Beyond the Degree: Strategic Positioning for Global Relevance in the AI Era.

    A ₦500 commitment fee secures a place, with an optional ₦1,000 for a certificate of completion. Dates for the next cohort are announced on the WhatsApp Community.

    AI Mastery

    Practical use of AI tools for study, research and work.

    Data analysis

    From zero to reading, cleaning and charting real data.

    Graphic design

    Layout, type and brand basics, built into a portfolio.

    Video editing

    Cutting, sound and export for social media and events.

    Every year

    Three programmes bring the whole community together each session.

    Annual Musabaqatul Qur’an

    Our annual Qur’an competition. Students register in the category that matches how much they have memorised, compete in a preliminary round, and the qualifiers recite at the grand finale. The fifth edition was held at the Ibrahim Alfa Hall on 9 May 2026.

    It is not only for huffaz. There is a category for anyone still building consistency, and every participant receives a certificate.

    Categories and how to take part

    Da’wah Week

    A week dedicated to Islamic outreach, learning, awareness and community engagement, run by the Da’wah team with lectures and activities across the week.

    Dates and the programme for the week are announced on the WhatsApp Community and Instagram.

    See the events calendar

    Questions about Islam can be sent in anonymously at any time and are answered in the community’s question-and-answer sessions.

    Annual Ramadan Lecture and Iftar

    A Ramadan programme featuring lectures, spiritual reflection, a community gathering and a shared Iftar meal.

    Through the month, AMSC also arranges iftar for students who stay on campus, taraweeh, tafsir sessions and a Zakat al-Fitr drive.

    The month’s iftar for students on campus is funded by members and alumni. Every contribution feeds a fasting student.

    Support Ramadan iftar

    Also running

    Smaller circles and activities that run alongside the main programmes.

    Musaffa

    A Qur’an recitation and correction circle on Wednesdays between Maghrib and Isha at the Male Hostel Mosque. It moves steadily through the Qur’an from Surah Al-Baqarah, and the week’s stopping point is posted to the community so nobody arrives lost.

    Sports

    Football for the brothers at 4:30pm on fixture days at the MTC Field, and indoor games for the sisters alongside the Circle. During major events the fixtures become an inter-department tournament.

    Islamic Q&A

    Questions about Islam are submitted anonymously, then answered in a session on the community group. Only questions about Islam are taken, and every one gets an answer.
