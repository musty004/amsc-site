    Dawrah

    Short, focused courses in Islamic knowledge. This term: Dawratul-Hadith, the 40 Hadith of Imam An-Nawawi, explained in depth.

    Registration open This term

    Dawratul-Hadith

    The 40 Hadith of Imam An-Nawawi, explained in depth.

    Where
    AFIT Masjid
    When
    Between Maghrib and Isha. The days will be announced soon.
    Who
    Brothers and sisters, together in one class, whatever your level
    How long
    About one month
    Book
    The text will be shared in the portal. Coming soon.

    How it works

    1. Register with the form on this page.
    2. Create your portal login with the same email when the Dawrah begins.
    3. Attend at the masjid. Missed a class for a valid reason? Listen to the recording in the portal.
    4. Answer two short questions after each class, then take the final exam.

    Only register if you can commit. The Dawrah is for students who want to go deep in their knowledge.
