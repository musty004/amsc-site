    Join AMSC

    Register once to become a member and be added to the AMSC WhatsApp Community. It takes about three minutes.

    Registration form

    Fill in the form below. Once you have registered, you will be added to the AMSC WhatsApp Community, our hub for announcements, academic updates, clearance guides and peer support.

    Form not loading? Open it in a new tab (opens in a new tab).

    What happens next

    1. You submit the formA copy of your answers is sent to your email.
    2. You are added to the WhatsApp CommunityAn AMSC admin adds you using the number you gave.
    3. You receive every updateProgrammes, tutorials, events and announcements.

    What membership gives you

    • Weekend tutorials and the past question bank
    • Islamiyya, Musaffa and the Ta’alim circles
    • Welfare officers to help you settle in
    • Attestation letters for scholarship applications
    • Mentorship from senior students and alumni
    • Priority places on IDIT cohorts

    Freshers’ guide

    Semester dues

    Dues are ₦1,000 and above per semester. They pay for Islamiyya materials, tutorial venues and printing, and events like the Musabaqah.

    If dues are difficult for you this semester, your membership continues unchanged. Nobody is removed for not paying.

    Questions

    Do I have to be very knowledgeable to join?

    No. Islamiyya exists because people start from different places. Beginners’ Qur’an reading is one of our best-attended classes.

    Is AMSC tied to one school of thought?

    No. AMSC serves every Muslim student at AFIT. Differences of opinion are discussed with adab and never used to exclude anyone.

    How do I get into the WhatsApp Community?

    Fill in the registration form above. An AMSC admin adds you with the phone number you give.

    Can sisters take part fully?

    Yes. The sisters’ wing has its own Circle, Ta’alim and leaders, and a share in every major event. See the sisters’ page.

    Can students from outside AFIT join anything?

    The IDIT training cohorts are open to people outside AFIT. Campus programmes are for AFIT students.
