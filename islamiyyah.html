    Islamiyyah

    Weekend Islamic education for AFIT students: Qur’an, Arabic and Islamic book studies, taught by students for students.

    2026/2027 session Prepared by the Islamiyyah Committee

    Learn, improve and strengthen your connection with Islam

    The Islamiyyah gives students structured Qur’anic, Arabic and Islamic studies alongside their regular academic work.

    Improve your recitation and memorisation, build your Arabic, and study foundational texts on belief, worship and the life of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him). Classes are grouped by ability, so a complete beginner and a student completing their Hifz both learn at the right level.

    • Saturdays and Sundays
    • 9:00am to 12:30pm
    • AFIT Mosque
    Student Portal Ask the Mudeer a question (opens in a new tab)

    Vision

    To develop students with strong Qur’anic knowledge, sound Arabic ability, understanding of Islamic teachings, and excellent character.

    Mission

    To provide consistent weekend Islamic education through Qur’an, Tajweed, Hifz, Arabic, Aqeedah, Fiqh, Seerah and Islamic studies, while encouraging discipline, good manners, responsibility, peer learning and continuous personal improvement.

    Weekend timetable

    Every class holds at the AFIT Mosque.

    DayTimeSessionWho
    Saturday9:00am to 11:00amMusaffa, Tajweed and HifzGrouped by Qur’an level
    Saturday11:00am to 12:30pmArabic LanguageGrouped by Arabic level
    Sunday9:00am to 11:00amMusaffa, Tajweed and HifzGrouped by Qur’an level

    Qur’an: Musaffa, Tajweed and Hifz

    Saturday and Sunday, 9:00 to 11:00am. Sessions cover recitation, Tajweed, memorisation and revision, and follow each student’s progress.

    • BeginnerFoundational Qur’an reading and basic improvement
    • IntermediateContinued memorisation, recitation and Tajweed
    • Advanced / HifzHigher-level memorisation, revision and recitation

    Arabic Language

    Saturday, 11:00am to 12:30pm, using دروس اللغة العربية (Durus al-Lughah al-Arabiyyah). The aim is functional Arabic that opens up Islamic texts.

    • BeginnerBasic Arabic language
    • IntermediateReading, comprehension and language development
    • AdvancedHigher-level Arabic and engagement with Islamic learning
    Download Durus al-Lughah al-Arabiyyah (PDF, 4.9 MB)Book 1, by Dr V. Abdur-Raheem · 127 pages · PDF, 4.9 MB

    Islamic Book Studies

    Taught by students, for students

    AMSC students teach and coordinate the Islamiyyah themselves.

    Those who have learned share beneficial knowledge, support fellow students in their Qur’an, help those who need extra guidance, and grow in confidence presenting Islamic knowledge. If you can help teach, tell the Mudeer.
